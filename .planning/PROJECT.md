# HW Team Website — Project Context

## Vision
The face of the HW Team agency on the web. A company site that sells the agency's capability the same way HW Team sells its clients' products: clear positioning, proof, and a single obvious next step (contact/quote).

## Who is HW Team
A digital product agency based in Thailand. We design and build conversion systems for e-commerce and digital product brands:
- Sales pages & funnels (Dr.Jade, Barbestro, B-Garlic, Veggies Balance)
- E-commerce with 3-step COD checkout (shop.drjadeofficial.com)
- LMS / digital product delivery (learn.aiflowlab.co, Classroom 24)
- Websites / CMS, integrations & automations (n8n, AI flows)
- Platform work (Networkz, Z Intelligence)

Team: Techatham Nakinkul (lead), Krit (full-stack), Pathomphop (data engineer), plus the Rook DS Eng Squad.

Build v1 company site:
- Home: who we are, positioning, hero + CTA

- Portfolio / case studies: Dr.Jade, Barbestro, Bossnoi, Camellia, Networkz Platform, Z Intelligence, Digital Product + LMS
- Team section + contact/quote form
